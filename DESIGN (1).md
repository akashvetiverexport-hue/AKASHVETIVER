## Brand & Style

The brand balances scientific precision with raw botanical potency. It serves health-conscious professionals, biohackers, and mindful wellness enthusiasts seeking clean, plant-derived supplementation backed by empirical research.

The visual style merges organic minimalism with high-end editorial clarity and targeted glassmorphic accents:
- **Atmospheric Depth:** Deep evergreen foliage layers, soft botanical backplates, and subtle diffused lighting cultivate immersion.
- **Scientific Restraint:** Crisp modular cards, sharp geometric framing, and clean monochromatic containers bring laboratory-grade order to lush organic backdrops.
- **Tactile Purity:** Translucent frosted overlays simulate polished laboratory glass while letting vibrant chlorophyllic textures show through.

## Colors

The color palette translates botanical vitality into a systematic hierarchy:

- **Primary (`#1E4D2B` - Deep Forest Green):** Anchors navigation, foundational brand cards, and primary emphasis across the interface.
- **Secondary (`#76A035` - Vibrant Moss):** Accents positive states, metric callouts, verification badges, and energetic micro-interactions.
- **Tertiary (`#C8DB9E` - Soft Sage / Herb Leaf):** Provides muted fills, pill chip backgrounds, soft badge states, and organic contrast.
- **Neutral (`#111612` - Deep Obsidian Forest):** Grounding carbon-black used for crisp high-contrast cards, primary button surfaces, and high-legibility display copy.
- **Canvas & Surface Tones:** Off-white herbal porcelain (`#F8FAF7`) serves as the base light surface, while pure white (`#FFFFFF`) isolates cards and imagery. Glass containers utilize white with 12% to 24% alpha over photographic foliage.

## Typography

The type system blends contemporary technical geometry with warm, humane legibility.

- **Headline (`Outfit`):** Geometric, wide-aperture display forms provide crisp modern punch against lush photographic canvases. Large display scale headings leverage alternating font weights (light italics paired with semi-bold weights) to evoke an editorial lifestyle tone.
- **Body & Label (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, open letterforms ensure effortless scanning across dense supplement ingredient decks, nutritional claims, and technical dosage charts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to an 8-column layout on tablet and a single-column layout on mobile.

- **Hero Canvas Structure:** Combines an expansive atmospheric visual stage (columns 1–12) with anchor metric cards and modular feature trays positioned at the bottom perimeter.
- **Section Margins:** Generous screen margins (`3rem`) preserve whitespace around high-impact hero compositions, compressing down to `1.25rem` on mobile.
- **Rhythm:** Internal component paddings strictly observe multiples of 8px, utilizing `space-md` (`1rem`) for dense metric modules and `space-xl` (`2.5rem`) for open content tiles.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on structural contrast, glassmorphism, and ambient botanical shadowing:

- **Level 0 (Atmosphere):** Rich photographic or ambient green gradient backdrop.
- **Level 1 (Dock & Trays):** High-contrast planar cards. Solid pure whites (`#FFFFFF`) and rich obsidian blacks (`#111612`) set directly against the natural background create stark modern definition without heavy drop shadows.
- **Level 2 (Glassmorphic Floats):** Floating badges, navigation bars, and verification stamps use translucent overlays (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` or `rgba(17, 22, 18, 0.4)`), backed by a `16px` backdrop-filter blur and a hairline `1px` inner border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Shadow Quality:** Where elevation shadows are necessary (such as isolated bottle packshots and dropdown menus), shadows are wide and deeply diffused with a subtle moss tint: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(17, 34, 20, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design system pairs clean, structural rectangular cards with softly rounded interactive elements and pill-shaped badges:

- **Containers & Content Tiles:** Feature disciplined 0.5rem (`roundedness: 2`) corners, with bottom anchor panels maintaining clean rectilinear alignments to anchor the layout.
- **Badges & Micro-elements:** Utilize full pill geometries for certification seals, category filters, and dosage tags to communicate organic softness.
- **Interactive Buttons:** Range from crisp slightly softened squares (`rounded-sm`, 4px) to full pills depending on call-to-action priority.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid neutral black (`#111612`) or deep forest (`#1E4D2B`) background with pure white typography, accompanied by an upward diagonal arrow icon. Minimal 4px corner rounding.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Glassmorphic translucent button or crisp 1px border in sage green with subtle hover tinting (`rgba(118, 160, 53, 0.08)`).
- **Text Action:** Underlined bold link with negative margin arrow indicator, rendered in `#111612`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Pill Badges:** Fully circular border-radius, rendered in soft sage (`#C8DB9E`) text over forest green, or vibrant moss green (`#76A035`) with white checkmark icons for certifications and purity guarantees.
- **Social Proof Counter:** Compact pill cluster grouping overlapping user avatar thumbnails next to bold numeric statistics (e.g., `+14K`) and clean micro-label copy.

### Cards & Trays
- **Information Tray:** Anchored modular card units dividing assessment CTAs, scientific formula highlights, and community validation into contrasting white and obsidian black segments.
- **Product Tiles:** Ultra-clean off-white backgrounds framing high-definition supplement packaging surrounded by living botanicals.

### Inputs & Selectors
- **Fields:** Crisp 1px border (`rgba(30, 77, 43, 0.2)`), white or frosted fill, high-contrast placeholder text with smooth forest green active focus rings.
- **Step Indicators:** Fine-line segmented progress bars showing active formula stage completion with smooth green fill states.
