Good to know.

A few things before you choose.

What are the products made from?

Our crafts are made using real vetiver roots, also known as khus or vettiver. Depending on the design, pieces include decorative thread, flowers, ribbon, beads or bells.

How do I place an order?

Choose a product and select “Request this piece”. Share your quantities and contact details. We’ll discuss availability, pricing and delivery with you on WhatsApp before you confirm or pay. Until online submission is connected, the form lets you prepare your request for email.

Can I request a custom piece?

Yes. Tell us about the design or occasion you have in mind. We’ll discuss what we can make, the price and the time needed before you confirm.

Do you deliver outside Tamil Nadu?

We ship across India. Share your delivery location when you enquire so we can confirm shipping charges and timing for your order.

Can I read customer reviews?

Our customer stories section displays only feedback approved for publication. New feedback will be reviewed before it appears.
