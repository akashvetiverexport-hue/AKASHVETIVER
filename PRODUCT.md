## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS — no framework. Vanilla implementation chosen by the user for simplicity and direct deployment.

## Users

Primary users are Indian consumers (all-India shipping) looking for handcrafted vetiver root (khus/vettiver) products for:
- Temple pooja and religious ceremonies
- Weddings (vetiver garlands / malai for bride and groom)
- Home decoration (door hangings / toran, curtains, wall pieces)
- Cultural gifts and souvenirs
- General purchase of traditional Tamil vetiver crafts

The buyer is typically familiar with vetiver's cultural significance in Tamil and South Indian traditions — its use in ceremonies, its natural cooling fragrance, and its auspicious meaning. Some buyers discover it as a unique, eco-friendly artisanal product.

## Product Purpose

Vettiver is an ecommerce catalog that showcases and sells handcrafted vetiver root products made by a family business. Visitors browse product photos, see prices and details, then place orders via WhatsApp. There is no online checkout — WhatsApp is the sole order channel.

Success means: a visitor finds a product they want, understands the price and craftsmanship, and taps the WhatsApp button to order it directly.

## Positioning

Family-made, handcrafted vetiver root products — not mass-produced. Each piece is made from real vetiver roots (khus khus) by the seller's own family. This is artisanal craft with cultural authenticity, not factory output. The natural fragrance and traditional Tamil craftsmanship are the primary differentiators.

## Operating Context

- Customers browse the website on mobile or desktop
- Product catalog with photos, prices, and descriptions
- No cart or online payment — ordering happens through WhatsApp chat
- WhatsApp link pre-fills a message with product interest
- Products include: vetiver garlands (malai) with decorative thread work, door hangings (toran) with flower embellishments, decorative peacock figures (mayil), ceremonial items (vel/spear, flags), door curtains with vetiver root fringe, and circular hanging decorations with bells
- Product photos are the primary sales tool — real photographs of actual handcrafted items

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no backend or database
- No payment gateway — WhatsApp is the checkout
- Product catalog is managed by updating HTML directly
- WhatsApp business number needs to be configured (currently placeholder)
- All product images are real photographs taken by the seller
- No user accounts, no reviews system (reviews section is planned as static testimonials)

## Product Principles

1. **Show the craft, sell the story** — Every product photo tells the story of handmade artisanship. The website is a window into the maker's workshop, not a generic store.
2. **Friction-free ordering** — The path from "I want this" to "I'm talking to the seller" should be one tap on WhatsApp. No registration, no cart, no payment forms.
3. **Cultural authenticity** — The products carry Tamil cultural meaning (weddings, temples, festivals). The website must respect and reflect this heritage without reducing it to decoration.
4. **Let the product lead** — Real product photography is the primary persuasion tool. The website exists to present these images at their best with clear pricing.
5. **Mobile-first reality** — Most customers will discover and browse on mobile, likely arriving from a WhatsApp-shared link. The mobile experience is the primary experience.
